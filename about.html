Understanding The Cosmos

About Space & Cosmology

Space is an almost perfect vacuum, full of mysterious phenomena, dark matter, billions of galaxies, and infinite possibilities.

What is Outer Space?

Outer space begins approximately 100 kilometers (62 miles) above Earth at the Kármán line, where the atmosphere becomes too thin to support aeronautical flight.

Contrary to popular belief, space is not completely empty. It contains low-density particles including hydrogen and helium plasma, electromagnetic radiation, magnetic fields, neutrinos, dust, and cosmic rays.

Human Purpose

Why Explore Space?

Space exploration drives innovation, protects humanity, answers fundamental questions, and unifies global scientific progress.

Technological Innovation

Spin-off technologies from space programs include GPS navigation, water purification, MRI scanners, solar panels, and camera sensors.

Search for Extraterrestrial Life

Investigating liquid water on Mars, Europa, and Enceladus opens the search for microscopic or complex life beyond Earth.

Planetary Defense

Tracking Near-Earth Asteroids (NEOs) and testing asteroid deflection technologies like NASA's DART mission ensures Earth's safety.

Historical Journey

Timeline of Discoveries

Milestones that forever transformed humanity's place in the cosmos.

1609

Galileo's Telescope

Galileo Galilei pointed a telescope at Jupiter, discovering its four largest moons and proving not everything orbits Earth.

1957

Sputnik 1 Launch

The USSR launched Sputnik 1, the world's first artificial satellite, marking the beginning of the Space Age.

1969

First Moon Landing

Apollo 11 astronaut Neil Armstrong stepped onto the lunar surface, declaring "One small step for man, one giant leap for mankind."

1990

Hubble Space Telescope

Deployed into orbit, Hubble provided ultra-clear deep field imagery of deep space, galaxy formation, and black holes.

2021 - Present

James Webb Telescope & Artemis

JWST captures infrared views of the universe's earliest galaxies while Artemis prepares permanent human habitats on the Moon.
