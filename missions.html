Famous Space Missions

Pioneering spacecraft and heroic lunar landings that reshaped humanity's understanding of the universe.

NASA

Apollo 11

1969

The historic spaceflight that first landed humans on the Moon. Commander Neil Armstrong and Lunar Module Pilot Buzz Aldrin landed the Eagle on July 20, 1969.

Objective: Land human crew safely on the lunar surface and return them to Earth.
NASA / JPL

Voyager 1

1977

The farthest human-made object from Earth. Voyager 1 crossed into interstellar space in 2012 and carries the iconic Golden Record with messages from Earth.

Objective: Conduct flybys of Jupiter and Saturn, then study the boundary of interstellar space.
NASA / Mars Science Lab

Curiosity Rover

2011

A car-sized robotic rover exploring Gale Crater on Mars. Curiosity verified that ancient Mars possessed conditions capable of supporting microbial life.

Objective: Search for habitability, organic carbon compounds, and ancient water signs on Mars.
NASA / International

Artemis Program

2022 - Ongoing

NASA's ongoing human spaceflight program aiming to land the first woman and first person of color on the Moon and establish a sustainable lunar base camp.

Objective: Establish permanent human presence on the Moon and prepare for crewed missions to Mars.
NASA / ESA / CSA

James Webb Telescope

2021

The premier space observatory of the 21st century. Positioned at Lagrange Point 2, its 6.5m gold-plated mirror peers back 13.5 billion years into cosmological time.

Objective: Observe the first stars and galaxies that formed after the Big Bang and characterize exoplanet atmospheres.
